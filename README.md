> I compress speech and audio language models and measure what is lost, how much can be recovered, whether all speakers lose equally, and whether the encoder can be removed altogether.

## 🧑‍🔬 About me

- 🔬 **PhD researcher** in the School of Computer Science and Electronic Engineering, University of Essex: compression, distillation and fairness in speech and audio language models.
- 🏭 **AI Engineer (NLP) at Hivedome Ltd.** as the Associate on a Knowledge Transfer Partnership with the University of Essex (part-funded by UKRI through Innovate UK), building **ASK ITAS**, an agentic AI assistant for commodity trading.
- 📰 **Previously ML Engineer at Eidosmedia, Milan (2022–2025):** retrieval-augmented question answering and automatic newspaper pagination for news publishers.
- 🎓 **MSc in Artificial Intelligence, University of Bologna** (Distinction).
- 💬 **Ask me about:** speech LLMs, model pruning, knowledge distillation, RAG, and LLM agents.

## 🔭 Research themes

| ✂️ Compression | 🧪 Distillation | ⚖️ Fairness |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pruning layers of the Whisper encoder inside SLAM-ASR: how word error rate changes with encoder depth, and how much LoRA fine-tuning of the LLM recovers. | **CARD**: audio captioning with no audio encoder at inference. A pretrained audio teacher is distilled into a small projector and the language model. | Compression is usually reported as one average error rate. I measure how pruning changes recognition error for each demographic group of speakers. |
