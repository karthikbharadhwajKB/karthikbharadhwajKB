## Hi, I'm Karthik

I'm a PhD researcher at the University of Essex. I work on speech and audio language models: how to connect audio to large language models, and how to make them smaller and cheaper to run.

### Research

**CARD: Cross-component Audio Representation Distillation for Encoder-Free Audio Captioning** (IEEE SLT 2026)
Audio captioning without a separate audio encoder.
[Code](https://github.com/karthikbharadhwajKB/CARD) · [Models](https://huggingface.co/collections/KarthikKB1998/card-encoder-free-audio-captioning-ieee-slt-2026-6aab0d5d46f574fd376ff1d7) · [Demo](https://huggingface.co/spaces/KarthikKB1998/CARD-Audio-Captioning)

### Projects

- [PaperReady](https://github.com/karthikbharadhwajKB/PaperReady): web app that cleans LaTeX projects for arXiv and checks ACL papers with aclpubcheck.
- [Agents](https://github.com/karthikbharadhwajKB/Agents): AI agents on the OpenAI Agents SDK, with Gmail, Notion, YouTube, and an MCP server.
- [Langchain_OpenSearch_Hybrid_search](https://github.com/karthikbharadhwajKB/Langchain_OpenSearch_Hybrid_search): hybrid (keyword + vector) and full-text search for LangChain's OpenSearch vector store.
- [Audio-LLMs](https://github.com/karthikbharadhwajKB/Audio-LLMs): prototype speech recognition model that connects a Whisper encoder to an LLM.
